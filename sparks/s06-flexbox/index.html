Strawberry lemonade

Ingredients

  • 1 cup fresh strawberries, hulled
  • Juice of 2 lemons
  • 3 tablespoons sugar, or to taste
  • 3 cups cold water
  • Ice and lemon slices for serving

Directions

  1. Mash the strawberries in a bowl until they release their juice.
  2. Stir in the lemon juice and sugar until the sugar dissolves.
  3. Pour the mixture through a strainer into a pitcher, pressing the berries to get their juice.
  4. Add the cold water and stir. Taste and add more sugar or lemon if needed.
  5. Serve over ice and garnish with lemon slices.

Tomato-basil pasta

Ingredients

  • 8 ounces pasta
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 2 cups chopped ripe tomatoes
  • 1 small garlic clove, minced
  • ¼ cup fresh basil leaves, torn
  • ¼ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • Salt and black pepper

Directions

  1. Bring a pot of salted water to a boil. Add the pasta and cook according to the package directions.
  2. While the pasta cooks, warm the olive oil in a pan over medium heat.
  3. Add the tomatoes and garlic. Cook for 6–8 minutes, stirring now and then, until the tomatoes soften.
  4. Drain the pasta, saving a small splash of its cooking water.
  5. Toss the pasta with the tomato mixture, basil, and a little cooking water. Season with salt and pepper.
  6. Serve with Parmesan sprinkled on top.

Pumpkin cookies

Ingredients

  • ½ cup softened butter
  • ¾ cup sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup pumpkin purée
  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. In a large bowl, cream the softened butter and sugar until smooth.
  3. Mix in the egg and pumpkin purée.
  4. In another bowl, stir together the flour, cinnamon, and baking powder.
  5. Add the dry ingredients to the pumpkin mixture and stir just until combined.
  6. Spoon the dough onto the baking sheet, leaving room between cookies.
  7. Bake for 12–14 minutes. Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet before serving.

Chocolate mug cake

Ingredients

  • 4 tablespoons flour
  • 2 tablespoons cocoa powder
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • ⅛ teaspoon baking powder
  • 3 tablespoons milk
  • 1 tablespoon neutral oil
  • 1 tablespoon chocolate chips (optional)

Directions

  1. Choose a large microwave-safe mug so the cake has room to rise.
  2. Stir the flour, cocoa, sugar, and baking powder together in the mug.
  3. Add the milk and oil, then stir until there are no dry patches. Mix in the chocolate chips if using.
  4. Microwave for about 60 seconds. If the center is still very wet, heat for 10 seconds more at a time.
  5. Let the mug cool for a minute before eating carefully with a spoon.
