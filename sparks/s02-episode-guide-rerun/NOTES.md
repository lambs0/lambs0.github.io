# NOTES — S02 Episode Guide Rerun

## Intent
What I built, for whom, and why (2–3 sentences).
I rebuilt the episode guide and improved the visuals of the table and the colors. I did it for anyone interested in Gravity Falls to give them a little information on the first few episodes.
## AI use
I used the built-in Copilot. I gave it the "done" checklist and asked for a plan. I specified that I wanted it to make a new index.html file and also a styles.css sheet. I asked it to base the new episode guide off my previous one. I also asked it for suggestions on good color combinations. I asked it to thicken the borders on the table and to change the background color of the body, header, and footer.
## Accepted / changed / rejected
The agent changed a few of the colors to ones I didn't like, so I made my own changes. It added more space in the table cells, which I liked, so I kept that.
## Dig deeper
I dug deeper into "padding" and learned that it's the way you can control how much space is inside each table cell. I experimented with it by changing the value on the padding, and the space in the cells changed dramatically.
