Pumpkin cookies

A cozy, simple recipe for anyone ready to bake a soft batch of pumpkin cookies.

Gather your ingredients

Set out 1 cup of pumpkin purée, 2 cups of flour, ½ cup of softened butter, ¾ cup of sugar, 1 egg, and 1 teaspoon each of cinnamon and baking powder.

Mix, bake, and enjoy

Heat the oven to 350°F. Cream the butter and sugar, then mix in the egg and pumpkin. Stir in the dry ingredients, spoon the dough onto a baking sheet, and bake for 12–14 minutes.

Let the cookies cool before sharing.
