# NOTES — <Type Scale>

## Intent
I built a page with a murder mystery theme to showcase fonts. The type should feel like a murder mystery novel and slightly old-fashioned. Anyone with glasses should be able to read it without their glasses.  

## AI use
I used the free copilot inside of VS Code. I asked it to create a new sparks folder with an index.html and a notes folder and for it to give me a page with a murder mystery theme. I asked it to provide a few fonts that might fit with this theme. I provided the done list and asked it to check over all items to make sure I didn't miss anything.

## Accepted / changed / rejected
I rejected the color the AI put onto the page. I wanted it to be black and white to keep the focus on the font. I rejected the suggestion that I use Playfair Display as the font for the headings. It was a good font, but it didn't quite give the vibe I was going for. I asked for more suggestions and chose Cormorant Garamond because it felt more like a mystery novel to me. I kept the overall layout and content the AI provided because I thought it aligned well with my intent.

## Dig deeper
I looked up font weight and learned that it's a way you can make a font bold. By changing the weight to a higher number, you make it bolder. https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/Properties/font-weight
