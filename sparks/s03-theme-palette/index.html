A cabin at dawn

A light snowstorm softens the forest just before sunrise. Snow gives the page a quiet, open feeling, while deep pine brings the warmth and shelter of a cabin. Frosted teal adds the cool color of distant trees, and a touch of dawn gold catches the first light.
