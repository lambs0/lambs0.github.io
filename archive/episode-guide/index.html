About Gravity Falls

Gravity Falls is a tv show that aired in 2012–2016. It's about twins who spend the summer in a town in Oregon called Gravity Falls. They notice strange things going on there and have many adventures during the summer. I like it because it's really funny, interesting, and nostalgic. It makes me want to visit Oregon and go on adventures of my own with my siblings.
