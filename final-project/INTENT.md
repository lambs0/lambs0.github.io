## Who it's for
My project is for my younger siblings in high school.Some questions the might ask me are "What is digital humanities? Is that something I would want to study in college? How do I know if it's for me?"


## My definition, first draft
The digital humanities is a field of study that uses technology to explore the humanities,which are different aspects of human culture and society. 

## Three examples
https://3d.si.edu/
This is the Smithsonoian Museum's site of 3D models of some of its artifacts. This fits my definition because the 3D models of different things really showcases the way technology was used to make parts of humanity and the knowledge we as humans have gaind more accessible to people. It would matter to my readers (younger siblings) because it serves as an example of a real application of digital humanities, and they could therefore know if they'd be interested in creating a similar thing if they decided to study digital humanities.

https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/
This project is an online exhibit that provides a lot of information about the Holocaust. It fits my definition because it explores a major part of human history in a digital format. It would matter to my readers because it's another example of something that could be done with studying digital humanities.

https://languagemap.net/
This project is a map of the languages of the world. It shows where they are spoken and provides a little information about each one. It also can show you extinct languages and sign languages. It fits my definition because it uses technology to explore a core aspect of human culture: language. I think this would matter for my readers because it would expose them to a way that technology can show the languages of the world.

## One Risk
One thing that's most likely to go wrong is that the format of the text gets messed up at 200% zoom (it overlaps or gets cut off). If this happens, I'll adjust the flexbox/grid elements. I'll make sure they use fr units and don't have a specific set width. I could use max-width rather than a set width. I could also use media queries so it can properly be displayed on both a laptop and a phone.
