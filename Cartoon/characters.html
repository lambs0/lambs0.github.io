Toad Patrol Characters

Fur Foot

Fur Foot is the oldest, and he's the leader of the toadlets. He is smart, brave, and loyal. He isn't afraid to put himself in harm's way to rescue one of his siblings. He carries an animal claw, which he frequently uses to cut things and climb up rocks and trees.

Panther Cap

Toad Patrol chalk art; George and Peggy Sarson/Teletoon. Art by Celestlynn Lambson. Used for school purposes only.

Panther Cap is the youngest of the toadlets but he's the most important. He is the only one who can hear the whispers of the Thunder Trees, which give directions to the fairy ring. Panther Cap is almost constantly in danger, but he trusts his siblings to rescue him. He carries an acorn that he keeps under his hat. Whenever the toadlets are lost, he spins the acorn like a top and it points in the direction they should go.

Beauty Stem

Toad Patrol chalk art; George and Peggy Sarson/Teletoon. Art by Celestlynn Lambson. Used for school purposes only.

Beauty Stem is the second oldest of the toadlets and acts as their mother figure. She is highly empathetic and often worries about the youngest toadlet, Panther Cap. She is a great singer and eventually learns how to become a healer. She carries a pouch of firefly dust, which can be used as a source of light or heat.
