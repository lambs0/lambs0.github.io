About the Show

Toad Patrol is a Canadian tv show about a group of toadlets on a journey to find the fairy ring, a magical portal that will transport them to the safety of Toad Hallow. It aired from 1999 to 2000 for season 1 and in 2002 for season 2. My brother and I watched this show on roadtrips when we were young. Its mysterious plot and lovable characters make it very memorable to me.
