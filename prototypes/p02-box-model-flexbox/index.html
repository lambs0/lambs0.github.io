Raspberry smoothie

Ingredients

  • 1 cup frozen raspberries
  • 1 ripe banana
  • ½ cup plain yogurt
  • ¾ cup milk
  • 1 tablespoon honey (optional)

Directions

  1. Pour the milk and yogurt into a blender, then add the raspberries and banana.
  2. Blend until smooth. Add honey if you want it sweeter, then blend briefly again.
  3. If the smoothie is too thick, blend in a little more milk. Pour into glasses and serve cold.

Horchata

Ingredients

  • 1 cup long-grain white rice, rinsed
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 4 cups water for soaking
  • 2 cups milk
  • ⅓ cup sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Ice and ground cinnamon for serving

Directions

  1. Put the rice, cinnamon stick, and water in a covered bowl. Refrigerate for at least 4 hours, or overnight.
  2. Remove the cinnamon stick. Blend the rice with its soaking water until the grains are finely broken up.
  3. Strain the mixture through a fine strainer into a pitcher, pressing gently to collect the liquid.
  4. Stir in the milk, sugar, and vanilla. Chill, then serve over ice with a little ground cinnamon.

Pork wonton soup

Ingredients

  • 8 ounces ground pork
  • 2 teaspoons soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil
  • 1 teaspoon grated fresh ginger
  • 2 green onions, thinly sliced and divided
  • 24 wonton wrappers
  • 6 cups chicken broth
  • 2 cups chopped baby bok choy

Directions

  1. Mix the pork with the soy sauce, sesame oil, ginger, and half of the green onions.
  2. Place about a teaspoon of filling in the center of each wrapper. Moisten the edges with water, fold over, and press firmly to seal.
  3. Bring the broth to a gentle boil in a large pot. Add the wontons in batches and cook until the pork reaches 160°F (71°C) in the center.
  4. Add the bok choy and simmer until tender. Ladle into bowls and top with the remaining green onions.

Chicken fajitas

Ingredients

  • 1 pound boneless, skinless chicken breast, sliced into strips
  • 2 bell peppers, sliced
  • 1 medium onion, sliced
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
  • 1 tablespoon fajita seasoning
  • 8 small flour tortillas
  • Salsa, lime wedges, or other toppings (optional)

Directions

  1. Toss the chicken strips with the fajita seasoning until evenly coated.
  2. Heat half of the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the chicken, turning it as it browns, until it reaches 165°F (74°C) in the thickest pieces. Transfer to a clean plate.
  3. Add the remaining oil, peppers, and onion to the skillet. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the vegetables are tender-crisp.
  4. Return the chicken to the skillet and stir until hot. Serve in warm tortillas with toppings, if desired.

Cream puffs

Ingredients

  • ½ cup water
  • ¼ cup butter
  • ½ cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 cup cold heavy whipping cream
  • 2 tablespoons powdered sugar, plus more for dusting
  • ½ teaspoon vanilla extract

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Bring the water and butter to a boil in a small saucepan. Remove from the heat, add the flour all at once, and stir until the mixture forms a ball.
  3. Let the dough cool for 5 minutes. Beat in the eggs one at a time until the dough looks smooth and glossy.
  4. Spoon 8 small mounds onto the baking sheet. Bake for 15 minutes, then lower the oven to 350°F (175°C) and bake for about 20 minutes more, until puffed and golden. Cool completely.
  5. Whip the cream with the powdered sugar and vanilla until it holds soft peaks. Split each cooled puff and fill with cream, then dust with powdered sugar.

Chocolate mousse

Ingredients

  • 6 ounces semisweet chocolate, chopped
  • 1 cup cold heavy whipping cream
  • 2 tablespoons powdered sugar
  • ½ teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Fresh berries for serving (optional)

Directions

  1. Melt the chocolate gently in a heat-safe bowl over a pan of barely simmering water, stirring until smooth. Set it aside to cool until just warm.
  2. Whip the cream with the powdered sugar and vanilla until it forms soft peaks.
  3. Stir a spoonful of whipped cream into the chocolate to loosen it. Fold in the rest of the cream gently until no streaks remain.
  4. Spoon into serving dishes and refrigerate for at least 2 hours. Add berries before serving, if you like.
