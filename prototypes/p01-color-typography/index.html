Why these fonts and colors

Barlow Condensed 700 gives headers a dominant yet simple look. Lora 400 makes the body text look professional and makes it comfortable to read. The combination of light colors like the foam and sea glass combined with darker colors like deeper water, kelp, and shore stone provide good contrast, which makes the text readable. These colors work together to create a marine-like atmosphere.

Laser tag party invitation

Visit the laser tag invitation

Why these fonts and colors

Orbitron 700 gives the headings a blocky look, which fits well with a laser tag theme. Inter 400 and 600 make the body text readable and simple. The color palette includes bright neon colors and dark backgrounds. This combination gives the feel of a laser lights in the dark while making the text readable.
